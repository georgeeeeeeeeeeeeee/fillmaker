# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-21

First release: VST3, AU and a standalone app for macOS 11+ (universal: Apple silicon and Intel),
VST3 for 64-bit Windows.

- GENERATE, regenerate and mutate drum fills, from plain rolls to cross-rhythms, ratchets and
  fills that fall apart on purpose, with a Behaviour slider from familiar to wild.
- Groove-aware: it hears the clip on its track and fits the fill to it.
- A piano roll to edit notes and velocity, with undo and redo.
- Plays in time with Live, looped or at the end of every 2, 4, 8 or 16-bar phrase.
- DRAG MIDI into Live, or export a MIDI file.
- Controls moved by Live's Key Map, MIDI Map or automation light up in the window; a control on
  another tab brings that tab up.
